# 01 — Brand Book

## 1. Product definition

**Subject.** Mariana Conrado, a photographer. The photos place her in Rio de Janeiro (inferred, not yet confirmed). She shoots
herself, her friends, and her city, mostly at night and mostly with the flash on.

**Audience.** Mariana first. Then the people in the photos, and whoever she
sends the link to. Nobody needs to be convinced of anything.

**The site's single job.** Give her photographs a room that feels like her.

**What it is not.** A portfolio pitch, a CV, a brand for a business, or a feed
replacement. No services, clients, press, follower counts, or calls to action
beyond "see more" and her Instagram.

**Positioning.** Instagram crops, compresses, and interleaves her work with ads.
This site shows it the way it looks when you're standing there: large, in the
dark, one at a time, in the order the night happened.

## 2. Brand idea

## **A diary kept with the flash on.**

A diary because the work is personal, dated, sequential, and not edited for
anyone. The flash because it's the one technical choice running through all of
it. It makes the night bright, close, and a little too honest.

## 3. Essence

**Direct flash.**

In photography, a direct flash is light pointed straight at the subject, with no
bounce and no diffuser. It's unflattering by textbook standards and alive by
every other standard. That is the brand: close, bright, honest, unretouched,
and not trying to be flattering.

## 4. Signature: the Reversal

Her name is her mark. `anairam` is `mariana` backwards, and the middle `i` is the
hinge that never moves.

- The wordmark rests as `anairam`, the way she writes it.
- Once per visit, on first load of the home, the letters travel to their
  mirrored positions and it reads `mariana`. Then it turns back.
- On hover or focus, it turns once more.
- The `i` stays put throughout. The pivot is her.

This is the brand's only animated identity element. It is not a loading
spinner, a repeated texture, or a pattern. See `04-UX-AND-MOTION.md` §3.2
for timing.

**Why it's right for her:** she invented it. We only noticed the hinge. It
also says the thing the brief says: she's smart, a little contrary, and it
takes a second look to read her.

## 5. Promise

You see her photos the way she sees the night.

## 6. Values

- **Unretouched.** Show the photo as it is: flash burn, tilted horizon, grain,
  no filters added by us.
- **In order.** The night happened in a sequence. Keep it.
- **Hers.** Her words and her pictures. We add a frame, never content.
- **Generous.** Friends are credited and asked. Warmth is the point.
- **Light on its feet.** Nothing on the site is heavier than the photos.

## 7. Personality

| She is | So the site is | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fascinating | Worth a second look, with a hidden hinge in the name | Mysterious for its own sake |
| Smart | Precise, sparse, one idea per screen | Clever-clever, full of easter eggs |
| Gracious | Warm credits, patient pacing, easy to use | Cute, twee, saccharine |
| Full of life | Flash, colour, night, movement with purpose | Loud everywhere at once |
| Deadpan (`>:(`) | Understated chrome, dry microcopy | Rude, ironic about her work |

## 8. Voice

There are two voices on the site, and they never mix.

### Hers: the captions

Verbatim, always. Lowercase, emoji, three languages, lyrics, literary
lines next to "é bafo né?!?!". We never edit, translate, summarise, or add to
them. A caption in English or Spanish gets `lang="en"` or `lang="es"` and stays
as written.

### The site's: the frame

Short, dry, pt-BR, lowercase, no emoji. It sounds like her bio: a raised eyebrow,
not a paragraph.

| Where | Write | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counter | `07 / 66` | "Photo 7 of 66" (that's the screen-reader text) |
| Next control | `próxima` | "Ver próxima foto ✨" |
| Index link | `tudo` | "Ver galeria completa" |
| About | `>:(` then whatever *she* writes | An invented bio |
| Empty state | `nada aqui. ainda.` | "Oops! Nothing to see" |
| 404 | `essa foto não existe >:(` | "Página não encontrada" |
| Instagram link | `@anairamodarnoc` | "Siga-me no Instagram!" |
| Footer | `fotos por mariana. site por benito, de presente.` | "© All rights reserved" |

Rules:

- One line where one line works. Never two where one works.
- No agency words: *portfolio, projeto, trabalho, criativa, experiência, universo*.
- No superlatives about her work. The photos make the case.
- The only emoticon the site owns is `>:(`, and it's hers.

## 10. Brand architecture in one line

> The room is dark. The photo is the light. The flash happens once. The name turns around.
